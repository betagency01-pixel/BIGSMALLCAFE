    Front page · Reviews

    Thane can't stop talking about its new favourite cafe-bar

    Guests across Google keep circling back to the same three things. First, a menu wide enough to need a map — flavourful pizzas, baos you won't find elsewhere and hearty noodle bowls — with cocktails and mocktails that hold their own.

    Second, the room. Reviewers call it vibrant, cozy and seriously aesthetic: a place that works for a first date, a birthday, a team night or a lazy afternoon with a cold brew.

    And third, the people behind the counter — warm, attentive and quick on their feet. Our sources say it makes the whole night feel easy. We can confirm.
